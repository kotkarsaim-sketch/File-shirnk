Compression Algorithms
01.

ZIP / DEFLATE

Uses LZ77 and Huffman coding. Frequent byte patterns are replaced with shorter codes — exactly what File-Shrink simulates at the word level.

02.

Huffman Coding

Assigns shorter bit sequences to more frequent symbols. The compression ratio above is a simplified Huffman estimate for the most frequent words.

03.

Dictionary Compression

Custom compression dictionaries map frequent strings to tokens. File-Shrink builds exactly that dictionary from your uploaded file.
